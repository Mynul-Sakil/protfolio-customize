# Project Information & Requirements

## Overview
A dynamic, responsive personal portfolio website with a built-in Content Management System (CMS) and secure Admin Panel. Designed to showcase a professional profile, work experience, services, skills, certifications, achievements, projects, and partnerships in a premium executive-style interface.

## Tech Stack
*   **Framework:** Next.js
*   **Database:** MongoDB
*   **Styling:** CSS / Styling solution of choice (Inspired by the provided demo style) and use tailwind. 

## Design & Aesthetics (Inspired by Demo)
*   **Style:** Premium executive-style interface, polished, and professional with a document/ledger motif.
*   **Colors:**
    *   Ink (Dark Blue): `#12233B`, `#1B3358`
    *   Parchment (Warm Off-White): `#F4EFE3`
    *   Page (Background): `#FBFAF6`
    *   Brass/Gold (Accents): `#AD8A46`, `#D9C48F`
    *   Slate (Muted Text): `#5C6B7A`
*   **Typography:**
    *   Headings: `Fraunces` (Serif)
    *   Body: `IBM Plex Sans` (Sans-serif)
    *   Accents/Labels: `JetBrains Mono` (Monospace)

## Visitor-Facing Website Sections
1.  **Home (Hero Section):** Professional photo, name, title, introduction, CTA buttons (CV, Contact, LinkedIn).
2.  **About Me:** Summary, career journey, mission/vision, personal values.
3.  **Experience:** Timeline, job roles, responsibilities, key achievements.
4.  **Skills:** Professional, technical, marketing, software/tools.
5.  **Services:** Business Development, Education Marketing, Student Recruitment, etc. Visitors can order a service via the contact form.
6.  **Projects / Portfolio:** Marketing campaigns, product development, IT projects.
7.  **Certifications:** Certificates, training, workshops, credential links.
8.  **Achievements:** Awards, event participation, conferences, photo gallery.
9.  **Partnerships:** Organisations worked with (e.g., Australian Polytechnic College, Monash Polytechnic College, One Career).
10. **Testimonials (Recommended):** Feedback from managers, clients, partners.
11. **Vision & Goals:** Long-term vision, leadership philosophy.
12. **Blog / Insights (Optional):** Articles on international education, marketing, etc.
13. **Resume / Downloads:** Downloadable CV, portfolio, brochures.
14. **Contact:** Contact form, email, phone, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, location.
15. **Footer:** Quick links, social icons, copyright, privacy policy.

## Admin Panel (CMS) Features
*   Update profile information
*   Upload and manage certificates and achievements
*   Add, edit, or delete work experience
*   Manage skills and services
*   Update contact information, email, phone number, and social media links
*   Upload a new resume/CV
*   Manage project portfolio and partner logos
*   View contact form enquiries
*   Control SEO settings and website content

## Non-Functional Requirements
*   Fully responsive (mobile-friendly)
*   SEO-friendly
*   Fast-loading
*   Secure
*   Scalable for future enhancements
